# paperclips

 - a paperclip will occasionally bounce across the botom of the screen, and settle for a bit before fading away. if clicked, it duplicates. if you click 10 times, they start replacing portions of the page (letters at first; then everything on the page that isn't its background, such as boxes, pictures, buttons, modals, the markov terminal and its robots, gets zapped into paperclips by a robot's eyes, innermost first; then the background is torn away in segments). The robots spread out across the screen rather than bunching up. After 15, then start reproducing on their own, until they take over the whole page. during this time, there's a shutdown button that appears somewhere on the screen, but it moves away from your cursor. A few seconds into the tearing, the first robot burns it into paperclips with a laser from its eyes, which takes over a second, so you can still click it in time (if the first robot is right next to the button, the nearest one that isn't fires instead). If you successfully click it, it does shut things down, the paperclips fall away, and the page returns to normal. Otherwise after the page is replaced, the page fades away and returns to its prior state
