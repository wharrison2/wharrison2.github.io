Best moves

    How it picks

    It tries the pieces in every order and every spot, clearing full rows and columns after each one, just as the game does. The best sequence is the one that takes the most squares off the board. On a tie, it picks the board with the least edge between filled and empty squares, so what's left is in solid blocks rather than scattered holes that new pieces can't fill.
