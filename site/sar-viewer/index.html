SAR pair viewer

Two Sentinel-1 radar images of the Weddell Sea, off the Antarctic Peninsula, taken four days apart. Compare them overlaid or side by side.

Red where the first image is brighter, cyan where the second is; gray where they match.

Point at a map for its coordinates. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024.

About

This is a static version of a viewer I wrote for finding pairs of Sentinel-1 images of the same spot, before and after an event. Both images are warped from their ground control points onto one polar stereographic grid, at 80 m a pixel, and shown in decibels with the same brightness scale.
