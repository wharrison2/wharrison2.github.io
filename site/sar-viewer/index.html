SAR pair viewer

Two Sentinel-1 radar images of the Weddell Sea, off the Antarctic Peninsula, taken four days apart. Compare them overlaid or side by side.

Background

Sea ice and cloud both look white in an optical photo, so here the radar shows much more than the photo does.

Red where the first image is brighter, cyan where the second is; gray where they match.

Point at a map for its coordinates.

About

This is a static version of a viewer I wrote for finding pairs of Sentinel-1 images of the same spot, before and after an event. Both images are warped from their ground control points onto NASA's polar stereographic map grid, at 64 m a pixel, and shown in decibels with the same brightness scale, over NASA's Blue Marble or that day's MODIS photo.
