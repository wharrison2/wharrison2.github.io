Anagrams

Type a word or phrase and see (some of) its anagrams.

About

A port of a C++ anagram finder I wrote. It filters for words that can be spelled from the phrase, groups them by letter count, then searches for groups that add to precisely your letters.

The dictionary has about 95,000 English words, including plenty of obscure ones, with rude words taken out.
